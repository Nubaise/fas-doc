## Backend Contract Used

The Student UI uses the existing backend APIs:

- `POST /api/v1/auth/login`
- `GET /api/v1/faculty`
- `GET /api/v1/faculty/:id`
- `GET /api/v1/departments`
- `GET /api/v1/availability-schedules/faculty/:facultyId/availability?date=YYYY-MM-DD`
- `POST /api/v1/appointments`
- `GET /api/v1/appointments`
- `GET /api/v1/appointments/:id`

Availability is generated by the backend from faculty schedules, exceptions, and existing appointments.

The frontend does not calculate appointment availability itself.

## Appointment Booking

Students select an exact backend-generated slot and provide an appointment reason.

The booking request contains:

- `facultyId`
- `startTime`
- `endTime`
- `reason`

The backend remains responsible for validating that the requested slot is actually available and for enforcing appointment business rules.

Students do not choose arbitrary appointment durations. Appointment duration remains controlled by the faculty availability configuration.
